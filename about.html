About Me

Who I Am

I'm Ekanola Adewole, a fullstack developer and founder based in Lagos, Nigeria. I build technology ventures that bridge the gap between enterprise-scale execution and entrepreneurial innovation — from AI-powered school management to coding bootcamps that train the next generation of African developers.

My journey into tech started with curiosity about how things work and has grown into building platforms that serve thousands of users across multiple countries. Every project I take on is driven by the goal of using technology to solve meaningful problems and create lasting impact.

Career Aspirations

I'm working toward becoming a leading voice in African tech — scaling ventures that combine education, AI automation, and youth empowerment. My goal is to build systems that create opportunities for young people across the continent.

  • Short-term: Expand Schoolwave to 100+ schools and grow Techill Academy.
  • Mid-term: Launch more AI-powered tools for African businesses and educators.
  • Long-term: Build an ecosystem of tech ventures that drive Africa's digital transformation.
